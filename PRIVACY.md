# FormDrop Privacy Policy

FormDrop is a hackathon project (ETHOnline 2026) that pays Google Forms
respondents automatically after an AI-judged, World ID-verified submission.

## What we access

- **Google Forms data**: with a creator's explicit OAuth consent, FormDrop
  reads that creator's form questions and response content (via the
  `forms.responses.readonly` scope) solely to detect new submissions and
  process the payout for each one. We do not read or modify any form the
  creator has not explicitly connected.
- **Respondent email address**: collected by the Google Form itself (via
  Google's own "collect email addresses" setting) and used only to send the
  one-time payout claim link.
- **Wallet and payment data**: Hedera testnet transaction data and Privy
  wallet identifiers, used only to execute and display the payout.

## What we don't do

We do not sell, share, or use this data for advertising. Form and response
data is used only to run the payout pipeline described above and is not
retained beyond what's needed to operate it.
